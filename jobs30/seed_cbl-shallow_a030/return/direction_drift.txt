  RECOMMENDATION ON BASE ANGLES -- PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED.
    Leave {0, 30, 60} as they are. Two reasons, and the second is the general one:
      (i) once compared at the window the nominal is right to within the error
          above, so there is nothing to compensate for;
      (ii) a drift that is the SAME across rungs cannot change coverage at all --
           shifting all 12 library directions by one constant leaves a uniformly
           spaced set of 12. Only a drift that DIFFERS between rungs moves the
           effective spacing, and that is what the per-rung column above is for.
    Revisit when the per-rung drifts span a usable fraction of the 30 deg bin.
    Samples so far: 1.

  === AND ONE THING FOLLOWS WITHOUT ANY FIT ===
  A drift that is one-signed and of order 10-20 deg by the time the window is
  sampled is NOT a spacing that 30-degree base angles deliver +/- 15 deg on.
  Twelve library directions at 30 deg give a worst-case gap of 15 deg only if the
  seeds sit where they were placed; a variable 10-20 deg one-signed excursion
  makes the worst case 25-35 deg, and on the DOMINANT SKILL AXIS.

  Projection (bin/pick_seed.py) removes the MEAN of that excursion and leaves its
  SCATTER, so it is a partial fix and cannot be the whole one. The honest fix is
  DENSER BASE ANGLES: 6 base angles at 15 deg = 24 library directions, worst-case
  7.5 deg before drift and ~15 after, which is what 3 angles were believed to give.
  Cost: 30 seeds instead of 15, i.e. ~86 GPU-h instead of ~43, against a corpus of
  ~1700 GPU-h -- 2.5% of the total to fix the axis the emulator is judged on.
  PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED.
