  WHAT GRID PASSES 'sub-grid fraction of sigma_w^2 < 40% at a 30 m receptor'?
  Measured on two grids (dz_sfc = 20 m and 8.56 m, both at dx=dy=30 m): the fraction
  collapses onto z/Delta and crosses 40% at z/Delta = 3.5-3.7. So Delta <= ~8.6 m,
  with Delta = (dx dy dz)^(1/3).

   dx=dy  dz_sfc  Delta  z/Delta  sg frac        cells   dt (s)  GPU h / sim h

    30.0    8.56  19.75     1.52    88.3%    1,007,400   0.0341           0.3  (as run, this grid)
    15.0    8.56  12.44     2.41    65.4%    4,029,600   0.0286           1.4
    10.0    8.56   9.49     3.16    46.5%    9,066,600   0.0234           3.9
    10.0    6.00   8.43     3.56    38.7%   12,877,200   0.0196           6.6  <- PASSES
     8.6    8.60   8.60     3.49    40.0%   12,227,250   0.0213           5.8  <- PASSES  (isotropic)

  A 3.5 h spin-up therefore costs 3.5x the last column:
    dx=10 m, dz_sfc=6.0 m:  23 GPU hours  = 35 chained 40-minute segments
    dx=8.6 m, dz_sfc=8.6 m:  20 GPU hours  = 30 chained 40-minute segments

  Refining dz ALONE cannot get there: with dx=dy=30 m, Delta <= 8.6 m needs dz <= 0.71 m,
  and at that anisotropy the horizontal filter (2*dx = 60 m) still cannot resolve the
  30 m eddies, so the Delta collapse itself stops being the right description.
