CONTAINMENT AT 2928 m WITH A 30 m RECEPTOR -- FIRST EVIDENCE, and it is not reassuring.

The deferred gate is "does the 80% source area resolve before the wrap cap". A smoke run of
the production estimator on runs/g24_ctlcbl/window (convective, flat, t_back 450 s, z_i
524 m, 120 particles per release over 300 s -- small, so read the SHAPE and not the third
digit) reports the integral as a function of how far a trajectory is allowed to travel:

   within 0.25 L  (  732 m)   integral 0.814
   within 0.50 L  ( 1464 m)   integral 0.951
   within 0.75 L  ( 2196 m)   integral 1.009
   within 1.00 L  ( 2928 m)   integral 1.043   <-- the production cap
   within 1.50 L  ( 4392 m)   integral 1.043   (unchanged: the cap binds, by construction)
   within 2.00 L  ( 5856 m)   integral 1.043

THE CURVE IS STILL CLIMBING AT 0.75 L -- +3.4% between 0.75 and 1.00 -- so influence has
NOT run out inside one domain length. The gate G2a passes trivially (the ratio past the cap
is 1.000 because the cap is what stopped it), which is exactly the failure mode that
tolerance was written to avoid reading as a pass: it tests that the CAP BINDS, not that the
footprint is contained. The quantity that answers containment is the slope approaching the
cap, and here it is not yet zero.

  For scale: Kljun on this map at z_m = 30 m puts x90 at 1173-1615 m over the
  corpus-eligible regimes, 40-55% of L. The LES footprint is broader than Kljun's, which
  is the whole reason the fourth pass measured an 80% source area of 3810 m at this
  receptor height in a 4380 m box.

WHAT IS NOT YET ESTABLISHED, and must be before any corpus:
  * this is a 450 s t_back; production is 900 s, which can only extend the tail
  * this is a convective window with z_i = 524 m; the neutral rung's footprint is longer
  * 120 particles per release over 300 s, against production's 700 over 1800 s

  So the number will move. The SIGN of the finding is unlikely to: at a 30 m receptor the
  2928 m box does not obviously contain the footprint, and the flat/neutral containment
  gate is where that gets settled. If it fails, the options are on the table and unchanged:
  accept the truncation and quote Kljun on the identical box; go to 146^2 @ 24 m (3504 m,
  ~+48% per case); or refuse near-neutral cases. That is a grid decision.

ALSO RECORDED, from the same run: the integral came out 1.043 against the physical ceiling
1 - z_m/z_i = 0.9456, i.e. 10.3% ABOVE it, on flat ground where no slope can explain it.
Two candidates, neither yet separated: the sigma_w floor (which inflates the near field --
measured at up to +18 points of array share on the retired closure) and the fact that this
window is a convective layer 1800 s old and still deepening, so z_i is a moving target in
the denominator of the asymptote. The production cases will have both a stationary seed and
a longer window; if the excess survives that, it is the closure.
