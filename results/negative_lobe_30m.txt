THE NEGATIVE LOBE AT A 30 m RECEPTOR: it is wind turning, and the CBL elevated-maximum
mechanism does not appear.  Measured 2026-08-29 on the two production targets, from the
persisted touchdown samples (unfolded, signed, 100,000 each).

WHY IT WAS WORTH ASKING. Fix 2 established that nothing clips the negative values and
measured 5.8-11.1% of |flux| across twelve 10 m convective footprints. The two mechanisms
that produce them sit in DIFFERENT PLACES, so the radial profile identifies which is acting:
an elevated concentration maximum in a CBL is NEAR-field (air arriving at the receptor from
above has not been in contact with the surface close in), wind turning with height is
FAR-field and off to one side.

THE NEAR FIELD IS NOT NEGATIVE. 0.1% and 0.0% inside 100 m, rising monotonically with
radius. There is no elevated-maximum signature, and it should not be expected: the receptor
sits at z/z_i = 0.023 (convective) and 0.030 (neutral), deep in the surface layer, where a
SURFACE source has no concentration maximum above the receptor to produce one. The whole
negative lobe is the wind-turning mechanism -- a parcel descending through an Ekman layer
arrives from a bearing rotated relative to the receptor-level wind, so its touchdowns land
to one side and, being the downward branch, enter the flux with the opposite sign.

WHAT THIS DECIDES FOR THE ML TARGET, and it decides it against the naive route.

  raster share of |flux|      2.32%  (convective)   2.03%  (near-neutral)
  TOUCHDOWN share of |weight| 20.9%                 35.0%

The raster hides an order of magnitude, because within a 24 m cell the positive and negative
touchdowns cancel before anything is written down. The target this project has chosen -- a
continuous density fitted to the points -- sees the uncancelled version, where a fifth to a
third of the mass is negative. A single non-negative normalizing flow cannot represent that,
and dropping the negatives would discard 21-35% of the signed mass, concentrated in the far
field, i.e. it would systematically shorten every footprint.

  The favourable half: the negative component is BROAD, SMOOTH and FAR-FIELD -- monotone in
  radius with no structure inside 200 m. A signed decomposition f = w+ p+ - w- p- therefore
  needs only a wide, simple p- , which is the cheapest of the three candidates in
  ML_TARGETS.md rather than the most fragile. Decide at build time, but decide knowing the
  number is 21-35% and that the shape is easy.
