VERDICT B3: the flat accuracy boundary is between CFL_3d 1.55 (k0/k1 0.132, clean) and
1.60 (8.511, REJECT). Take 1.575 as the boundary. Production CFL_3d = 1.3442
(dt = 0.0295858 s, 169 steps per 5 s dump) sits 17% below it -- above the 10% margin rule.

AND IT IS NOT THE RETIRED 24 m GRID'S NUMBER. That grid had the same anisotropy
(dx/dz_sfc = 2.804) and its boundary was ~1.64. This one is ~1.575, 4% lower. The margin
rule survives; the number it is applied to does not, which is exactly why PROJECT_BRIEF.md says
to re-measure the boundary at every grid rather than carry it.

The transition is SHARP -- 0.132 to 8.5 across one rung -- and the three clean rungs read
the same 0.132 to three digits because that is the converged value of a healthy field over
4000 steps, not because the test is insensitive: it moves by a factor of 64 at 1.60.
turb_alive says OK at every rung including the rejected ones, which is the point of running
it alongside: k0/k1 answers "is dt too large", turb_alive answers "is there turbulence at
all", and neither substitutes for the other.
