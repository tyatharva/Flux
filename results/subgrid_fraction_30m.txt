  AT THE RECEPTOR: 52.5% sub-grid, against the ~52% the reconfiguration predicted and
  against ~90% at the retired 10 m receptor. z/Delta = 1.76 (Delta = 17.05 m) against 0.99.

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT LEADING INDICATOR. The 10 m configuration failed because the near
field was closure output rather than LES output: at z/Delta ~ 1 the isotropic (2/3)e_sgs
partition sets sigma_w, so the peak could not respond to meteorology. The sub-grid fraction
is that statement as one number, and it is available from a control window before either
target runs -- one number instead of two more GPU-hours.

WHAT IT DOES NOT SAY. The closure is still doing real work. At this window's stability
(L = -85.4 m, zeta = -0.35) the MOST anchor is 1.25 phi_w = 1.589 and the LES delivers
sigma_w/u* = 1.016, 64% of it, so the floor is still active -- but its influence is now
WEIGHTED by f_sgs = 0.525 rather than by ~0.90, which is what changes. And this particular
window is a 900 s slice of a convective layer 1800 s old, still spinning up; the seed-based
control will be the fair number.

  window statistics: u* 0.419, sigma_w 0.425 m/s, h 524 m, L -85.4 m, U(30) 5.83 m/s

=== AND THE NEUTRAL NUMBER IS NOT THE CONVECTIVE ONE. Measured on the nbl-deep seed. ===

  regime        z/Delta   resolved sigma_w^2 at 30 m   sub-grid
  convective      1.76              47.5%                52.5%
  NEUTRAL         1.76              13.6%                86.4%

Same grid, same receptor, same Delta = 17.05 m. The difference is physical and docs/les/lpdm-and-footprint.md
already predicts it: the resolved fraction collapses onto z/Delta, crossing 40% at
z/Delta ~ 3.5 neutral and ~2.1 convective, because thermals draw on z_i-scale motions
while shear-driven eddies scale with z. At z/Delta = 1.76 convection is just past its
threshold and neutral is only half way to its own.

  Cross-check, and it is a good one: the FOURTH pass measured 85.5% neutral / 52.3%
  convective at a 30 m receptor on a 186^2 @ 24 m domain. This pass gets 86.4% / 52.5%
  on 122^2 @ 24 m -- a different domain size, a different surface, a different closure,
  and the same two numbers to within a point. The resolved fraction is a property of
  z/Delta and the regime, exactly as claimed.

  Against the retired 10 m configuration: neutral 96.4% -> 86.4% sub-grid (resolved 3.6%
  -> 13.6%, a factor of 3.8), convective ~90% -> 52.5%.

WHAT THIS PREDICTS ABOUT THE DECIDING TEST, said before the targets ran. The convective
target is out of the closure-dominated regime and the near-neutral one is not. If the peak
moves, it will move because the CONVECTIVE case moved; if the neutral case's peak is stuck
at the same value the 10 m cases were, that is consistent with 86% sub-grid and is not a
surprise. A 30 m receptor fixes the convective half of the corpus at this grid and leaves
the neutral half where it was.
