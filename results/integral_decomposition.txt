SEPARATING THE TWO INTEGRAL FAILURES.  2026-08-29.

THE QUESTION. The convective target integrated to 1.463 and the near-neutral one to 0.888
against a physical ceiling of 1 - z_m/z_i ~ 0.97. Truncation explains 0.888; it cannot
produce 1.463, because a finite backward time and a finite domain can only LOSE influence.
The two are different problems and the by-displacement decomposition separates them -- but
only if the wrap cap is RAISED, because production retires a trajectory at one domain
length and the curve past 1 L is then flat by construction.

CONVECTIVE: IT SATURATES.  +1.4% over the last quarter-domain before the cap, and +0.8%
over the whole two domain lengths past it. The cap is removing essentially nothing. The
integral settles at 1.043 = 1.067x the 1 - z_m/z_i ceiling, and since neither truncation nor
wrap can put it there, what is left is the advective non-closure -- w_bar at the receptor is
-0.141 m/s, a real mean subsidence over sloping ground, and docs/les/lpdm-and-footprint.md already states that
the turbulent flux there genuinely is not the surface flux. THAT IS PHYSICS AND IT STAYS.

NEAR-NEUTRAL: IT KEEPS CLIMBING.  +7.3% over the last quarter-domain before the cap and
+15.8% over the two lengths past it, still rising at 2.5 L. Every one of those touchdowns
is a trajectory that has travelled further than the domain repeats, so it is re-sampling
turbulence it already sampled: WRAP DOUBLE-COUNTING, and the cap is right to remove it.
The consequence is not that the cap is wrong -- it is that the capped 0.750 is a TRUNCATED
estimate of a footprint the box does not contain. Both containment gates fail:
