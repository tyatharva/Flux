  RECOMMENDATION ON BASE ANGLES -- PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED.
    Leave {0, 30, 60} as they are. Two reasons, and the second is the general one:
      (i) once compared at the window the nominal is right to within the error

           spaced set of 12. Only a drift that DIFFERS between rungs moves the
