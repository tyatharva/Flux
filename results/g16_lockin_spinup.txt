=== lock-in diagnostic: the 2-D spectrum of w at mid-depth ===
An unconstrained CBL peaks near lambda ~ 1.5 z_i. A locked one snaps to the
largest mode the box holds (mode 1, lambda = L) and that mode takes an
anomalous share of the variance.

  READ: lam/z_i near 1.5 and a small mode-1 share = unconstrained.
        peak lambda pinned at L, a large mode-1 share, and r(L/2) strongly
        negative = the box is organising the thermals.

The deep case IS locked in: the spectral peak sits at mode 1 (lambda = L exactly)
and that single mode carries 54% of the mid-depth w variance, against 2% in the
compliant case. A 25x difference, and not a marginal call.

This does NOT by itself decide Gate E. The 4 z_i rule was written for mixed-layer
similarity -- w* scaling, entrainment, the shape of the w spectrum at mid-depth --
and lock-in is precisely the failure of that. Whether it reaches a footprint at
10 m, where surface-layer scaling governs and Kljun's own z_i channel spans one
percentage point of array share, is the separate question the footprint comparison
answers. Because the artifact is demonstrably PRESENT, agreement there would be
strong evidence rather than a vacuous pass.
