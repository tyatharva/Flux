THE CRITERION, fixed now:

  1. |peak_A - peak_B| from the LES must EXCEED each target's own half-vs-half Delta-peak
     floor, measured on that case and not assumed. (The 30 m ensemble table's peak p90 is
     60 m, but that was measured on a 60 m raster over 12.5 minutes; this raster is 24 m
     and the averaging period is 30 minutes, so the measured floor is the tolerance and
     the 60 m is an upper bound on it.)
  2. The two LES peaks must ORDER the way Kljun's do: the convective case closer in.

  If both hold, the peak responds to meteorology and the configuration worked.
  If the peak does not move, the receptor is still closure-dominated and the
  configuration has NOT worked. Say so plainly and stop.

AND THE BAR IS NARROW, WHICH IS ITSELF A RESULT. Scored over all 70 corpus-eligible
candidate hours in results/selected_times_24m.tsv, Kljun's own x_peak at z_m = 30 m spans
only 103-169 m -- 66 m, 2.8 raster cells. That is the entire dynamic range the emulator has
to reproduce in this observable at this receptor height. A 41 m separation is 62% of the
full range, so this pair is a demanding but not extreme sample of it. The 126-206 m range
quoted in the geometry table is wider only because it includes stable and very stable
classes, which the corpus does not contain.
