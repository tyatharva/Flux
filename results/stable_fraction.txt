HOW MUCH OF THIS SITE'S STABLE RECORD IS WEAKLY STABLE?   z_m = 10.0 m AGL
======================================================================================

Ozmidov scale stays above Delta = 10.09 m. Measured on the collapsed seed, that
fails at z/L ~ 0.2. The band being tested is 0.03 < z/L <= 0.10, and everything
BELOW it is more runnable still, so the operative figure is z/L <= 0.10.
